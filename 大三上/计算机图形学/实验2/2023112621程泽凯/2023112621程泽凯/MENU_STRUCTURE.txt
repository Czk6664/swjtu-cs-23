菜单结构 - 需要在Visual Studio资源编辑器中手动添加

打开 WindowsProject2.rc -> Menu -> IDC_WINDOWSPROJECT2

建议的菜单结构：

IDC_WINDOWSPROJECT2 MENU
BEGIN
    POPUP "文件(&F)"
    BEGIN
        MENUITEM "新建(&N)",                    IDM_NEW
        MENUITEM "打开(&O)...",                 IDM_OPEN
        MENUITEM "保存(&S)...",                 IDM_SAVE
        MENUITEM SEPARATOR
        MENUITEM "退出(&X)",                    IDM_EXIT
    END
    POPUP "绘图工具(&D)"
    BEGIN
        MENUITEM "直线(&L)",                    IDM_DRAW_LINE
        MENUITEM "圆(&C)",                      IDM_DRAW_CIRCLE
        MENUITEM "矩形(&R)",                    IDM_DRAW_RECTANGLE
        MENUITEM "三角形(&T)",                  IDM_DRAW_TRIANGLE
        MENUITEM "菱形(&D)",                    IDM_DRAW_DIAMOND
        MENUITEM "平行四边形(&P)",              IDM_DRAW_PARALLELOGRAM
        MENUITEM "曲线(&U)",                    IDM_DRAW_CURVE
        MENUITEM "多义线(&Y)",                  IDM_DRAW_POLYLINE
    END
    POPUP "编辑(&E)"
    BEGIN
        MENUITEM "选择(&S)",                    IDM_SELECT
        MENUITEM "删除(&D)",                    IDM_DELETE
        MENUITEM SEPARATOR
        MENUITEM "移动(&M)",                    IDM_MOVE
        MENUITEM "旋转(&R)",                    IDM_ROTATE
        MENUITEM "缩放(&Z)",                    IDM_SCALE
    END
    POPUP "几何计算(&C)"
    BEGIN
        MENUITEM "求交(&I)",                    IDM_INTERSECTION
        MENUITEM "垂线(&P)",                    IDM_PERPENDICULAR
        MENUITEM "圆心(&C)",                    IDM_CENTER
        MENUITEM "切线(&T)",                    IDM_TANGENT
    END
    POPUP "帮助(&H)"
    BEGIN
        MENUITEM "关于(&A)...",                 IDM_ABOUT
    END
END

所有ID已在Resource.h中定义，直接在资源编辑器中使用即可。
