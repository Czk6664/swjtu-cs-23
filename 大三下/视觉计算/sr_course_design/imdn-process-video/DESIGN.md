## Style Prompt

Academic technical explainer for a Southwest Jiaotong University visual computing presentation. The video should feel precise, clean, and report-ready: white canvas, SWJTU blue structural accents, light-gray panels, crisp data labels, and restrained motion that demonstrates computation rather than decoration. Use animated pixel grids, feature-map channels, arrows, and equations to show the single-image super-resolution pipeline from LR pixels to HR detail reconstruction.

## Colors

- SWJTU Blue: `#0F6BC9` for primary headings, paths, and model blocks
- Deep Blue: `#083575` for dark emphasis panels and transition wipes
- Paper White: `#FFFFFF` for the main canvas
- Soft Gray: `#F5F7FA` for panel backgrounds
- Text Gray: `#333333` for body text
- Detail Amber: `#F5A623` for high-frequency/detail highlights
- Signal Green: `#20A66A` for successful reconstruction/evaluation indicators

## Typography

- Chinese titles and body: `Microsoft YaHei`, `SimHei`, system sans-serif fallback
- Data/equations: `Cascadia Mono`, `Consolas`, monospace fallback
- Use bold Chinese headings and tabular numeric code labels. Keep all text large enough for PPT embedding.

## Motion

- Medium-energy academic explainer motion: push/block transitions, short staggered entrances, and blue wipe covers.
- Use left-to-right flow for computation, vertical stacking for pixel/detail comparisons, and finite pulse/scan animations to indicate repeated convolution.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use decorative stock-photo or dark cinematic backgrounds.
- Do not overuse gradients or neon effects.
- Do not make the process feel like a marketing opener; this is a technical report insert.
- Do not hide equations in tiny text.
- Do not use random, time-dependent, or infinite animations.
